What is now covered

One traceable stock journey

The proposal joins purchasing, PO receiving, and the filler’s after-run return without creating parallel or duplicate stock movements.

1Return by completed runSelect the route/machine, record the item and count, and retain the source.
2Separate damaged stockGood products go to Warehouse; damaged products require a reason and go to Quarantine.
3Allocate every arrivalEach good PO unit is assigned to Online store, Vending machines, or a balanced split.

Integrated purchasing

1Buy listUse suggestions net of stock on hand and quantities already ordered.
2Supplier availabilityCapture prices, available quantity, MOQ, validity, and ranked substitutions.
3Release callsUse comparables, expected sell price, margin, deposit, and demand evidence for new sets.

Purchase-order editing and receiving

Inventory → Purchase Orders & Invoices

1Open the POView/edit order details and Pokémon line items, or go directly to Receive.
2Mark arrivalsUse the grouped delivery controls, or receive all outstanding; arrived includes damaged.
3Calculate goodGood = arrived − damaged. Show the quantity still due.
4Allocate goodOnline store, Vending machines, or an exact split of both.
5Confirm receiptWrite channel and Quarantine movements; set the PO Partial or Received.
Required balance: Online-store quantity + Vending quantity = Arrived now − Damaged. Confirmation stays blocked until it balances.

Inventory language

Four destinations with different meaning

The UI must never use one ambiguous “Store” balance for stock intended for different channels. Current stock defaults to 10 rows, offers 25 rows, and can be searched, filtered by language/allocation, and sorted without relying on an undefined stock-health graphic.

Online storeSellable stock allocated to ecommerce fulfilment.
Vending machinesSellable stock allocated to route staging and future fills.
Warehouse · unallocatedGood route returns waiting for a deliberate allocation.
QuarantineDamaged, non-sellable stock excluded from every sellable total.

Before backend sign-off

Five product decisions remain open

Mixed condition on one SKUCan one returned SKU be split into good and damaged quantities, or should staff add two lines?
Allocation of good returnsKeep route returns unallocated until a warehouse task, or allocate them during the return?
Return eligibility windowHow long does a completed run remain selectable, and can it have multiple return batches?
Physical locationsConfirm real shelf, route-staging, and Quarantine location codes.
Correction permissionsConfirm who may correct a confirmed receipt/return through compensating movements.
Current prototype decision: a returned row has one condition. Good route returns land in Warehouse · unallocated. These are safe proposal defaults, but production behavior should be confirmed.
