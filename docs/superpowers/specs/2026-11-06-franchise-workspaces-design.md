# Franchise workspaces

Approved by the user on 6 November 2026. Extend the existing static Game Guys portal, retaining its purple brand, Inter/Montserrat typography and franchise scope.

Use in-page workspaces for Training, scanning, picklist creation and order details; use a short dialog for stock adjustment. Popups for every flow would constrain training and orders; separate HTML pages would fragment the current navigation.

Stock uses compact expandable rows, search, filters, pagination and density controls, including a representative catalogue over 100 lines. Scanning supports manual barcode/SKU entry, a sample scan, results and unknown codes. Adjustments preview the new balance and require a reason. Training appears as a Home subtab with searchable categories, progress and readable lessons. Picklists support machine selection, manual or recommended quantities, review and draft creation without stock deduction. Shopify purchase records open read-only details with per-item quantity, cost, line total and proposed retail.

All data and mutations are browser-only demonstrations. No Shopify checkout, live inventory write, real video or camera recognition is implied. Preserve quarantine and exactly-once fill boundaries. Keep purchase order totals internally consistent. Publish to the existing GitHub Pages repository.
